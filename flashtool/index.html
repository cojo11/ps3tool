Information

This tool allows you to dump your PS3's flash memory, apply patches for CFW installation and get general usage info. Supporting firmware 4.80-4.93!


All operations (except for the optional Online Flash Verification feature, read our Privacy Policy. No data is stored in persistent storage) are conducted locally on your console. We do not store or share any of your consoles data!

For help with the tool, you can join our Discord server!
